# Article image requirements

Owner instruction, October 23, 2026: every blog article needs a different, relevant image, and the image itself must link to its actual original source. A canonical article URL is not an image source.

- Assign each article its own image ID and source in assets/blog-images.json. Do not reuse a category default, another article's image, or a crop of the same image as a supposedly new photo.
- Put the hero, descriptive alt text, dimensions and source link directly in the article HTML. The figure must work without JavaScript. Credit the source in a visible caption; link any required license.
- Keep useful technical diagrams when they add information. Link them to their original files too. Do not present illustrations as documented contractor projects.
- Original HomeCostEngine editorial illustrations link to their own full-size image assets. Stock photos link to the individual photo's source page, not a search-results page. Current sources: 20 original editorial assets, 50 Pexels photos (Pexels License), one Wikimedia Commons furnace image by Wtshymanski (CC BY-SA 4.0, unmodified file, fitted preview).
- Check the individual source and license before use. Being visible on Google is not permission. Do not use paid, watermarked or noncommercial-only images for this commercial site.
- Use the article's assigned image on its blog-index card. The title and Read article link still open the article; the photo links to its source.
- Synchronize og:image and Article.image with the hero. Do not change editorial reviewed dates merely for an image replacement.
- Before publishing, verify unique image identities, image loading, caption/source links, metadata, dimensions, mobile layout and relevant image-to-topic fit. Existing external stock images are loaded from their source CDN; retain the source record if an asset ever needs replacement.

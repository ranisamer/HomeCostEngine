Why buy this workbook?

Your practical output is one review packet joining the accepted baseline, proposed change, net price, decision evidence and later invoice. Use it when changes arrive during an existing project.

  • Show both additions and supported credits in a net-price bridge.
  • Check quantity changes, percentage bases and shared fees.
  • Keep accepted contract value, pending exposure and cash timing separate.
  • Match invoices to change IDs and identify duplicate billing questions.

Who should use it?

U.S. homeowners with an existing agreement and a specific proposed change. Bring the actual contract, revisions and provider evidence.

Who should skip it?

If you only need a quick check, start with the free change-order checklist and worked example. This is not a technical design, legal contract, professional valuation or substitute for local advice.

Overlap with existing books

The Contractor Quote Comparison Playbook and general renovation books already introduce change budgets. This workbook adds a focused revision-to-invoice record set and three new cases. Buying both is optional.

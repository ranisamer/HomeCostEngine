What you leave with

Complete one matched solar-offer comparison, with unresolved items assigned to the installer, lender or utility. Keep the cash offer, financing agreement, energy model and utility bill scenario distinct.

  • Match complete cash scope and retain the original headline price.
  • Separate DC capacity, production estimates, guarantees and utility-bill assumptions.
  • Reconcile loan payments and compare lease/PPA escalation on an explicit basis.
  • Record incentive evidence, service and transfer questions before commitment.

U.S. homeowners holding actual rooftop-solar proposals. Bring complete installation and payment documents, utility bills and the relevant tariff.

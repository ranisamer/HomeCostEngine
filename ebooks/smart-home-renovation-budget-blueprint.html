Try the method before buying

Suppose your defined work is $18,000, selections are allowed $2,000 and owner costs are $1,000. A chosen reserve of 10% of work plus allowances is $2,000, making the all-in plan $23,000. If your ceiling is $22,000, resolve the $1,000 gap before committing. These are hypothetical USD amounts, not market prices or a recommended reserve percentage. The book shows how to record the basis, replace assumptions with local quotes and avoid counting paid invoices twice.

Check your scope with the free project contingency calculator, then compare matched proposals in the contractor quote comparison calculator. You can use these tools without purchasing the book; the paid PDF adds the complete sequence, worked cases and reusable records.

What you can prepare

Prepare a selected-load outage brief, an evidence-based list of provider questions, two clearly labeled runtime scenarios and a matched all-in budget. Leave technical sizing and installation approval to qualified local professionals.

  • Identify the services you want during an outage, with input sources and unresolved technical questions.
  • Separate output power from stored energy, starting charge and conditional duration.
  • Ask how recharge timing and supporting work change a proposal.
  • Match complete installed scope and keep warranty/service limits visible.

Who is this for?

U.S. homeowners reviewing stationary battery-backup proposals with qualified providers. Bring actual proposals, relevant household requirements and available records.

Try the distinction

In an invented no-recharge scenario, 8.40 kWh available to AC loads divided by a constant 0.70 kW gives 12 hours; 1.40 kW gives 6 hours. These quotients assume compatible power capability and the stated starting energy. They are not a real home's performance forecast.

What is inside

A full project-planning workflow, not a short checklist.

The book is structured as 24 planning modules. Each module includes a current-planning page, a scope worksheet, a contractor quote check and a decision/action page. The final 20 pages are reusable records for measurements, allowances, risk, schedule, payments, warranties and closeout.

Why buy this painting guide?

A room price can hide different surface areas, preparation and coating assumptions. This guide helps you define the finish and compare the complete work before committing.

  • Check the paint arithmetic. Separate wall area from floor area, opening deductions, coats, coverage and primer.
  • Make preparation visible. Record assessed conditions, allowances, protection, access and occupied-room needs.
  • Compare the same finished scope. Match surfaces, products, coat assumptions, labor units and exclusions before ranking proposals.

Who will get the most from it?

U.S. homeowners planning painting work or comparing professional painting proposals. Use measured surfaces, actual product documents and qualified provider assessments.

Why buy this roofing handbook?

A low headline bid can leave you guessing about removal, deck repairs, flashing and disposal. This book helps you turn those questions into a clear comparison before you commit.

  • Understand what the bid really includes. Work through the assembly, interfaces, access and exclusions with each bidder.
  • Make hidden-condition costs visible. Record the deck-repair unit, allowance, evidence and approval process before tear-off.
  • Follow the money through closeout. Track payments, credits, changes and warranties without rebuilding your records from scratch.

Who will get the most from it?

U.S. homeowners planning a roof replacement or reviewing competing roofing proposals. It is especially useful before signing, when there is still time to clarify scope. It does not teach DIY installation or replace a professional roof assessment.

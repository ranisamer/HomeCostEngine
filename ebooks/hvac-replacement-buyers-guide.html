HomeCostEngine field guides are designed to sit beside measurements, contractor proposals and the free calculators on this site. The goal is not to give you one magic price. It is to help you define scope, identify assumptions and ask better questions before money is committed.

Use this guide for personal planning and pair it with the relevant HomeCostEngine calculators and cost guides.

Inside the 104-page edition

The buyer's guide is structured around complete installed scope rather than equipment price alone. It helps you document comfort problems, ask how the system was sized, compare matched equipment, understand ductwork and controls, and confirm what commissioning, permits, warranties and startup work are actually included.

The PDF contains 20 planning modules. Every module includes a concept page, a scope worksheet, a contractor-quote check and a decision/action page. The final 20 pages are reusable worksheets for measurements, allowances, risk, schedule, change orders, payments, warranties and closeout.

What this book does - and does not - replace

The guide is for homeowner planning, budgeting and contractor conversations. It does not replace a site inspection, contractor proposal, engineering design, legal advice, manufacturer installation instructions, permit review or local code requirements. Use the worksheets to record those project-specific answers when you receive them.

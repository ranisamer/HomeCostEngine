Roof underlayment cost

Underlayment is mostly hidden after the roof is finished, but it is a core part of water management. Compare product, coverage and installation details—not just a generic allowance.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

Ask what product is included

“Underlayment included” is not enough. Ask for the manufacturer and product type, where it will be installed and whether separate self-adhered leak-barrier materials are included where required or specified.

Coverage is not always equal to roof area

Overlaps, detail zones, valleys and waste can make ordered quantity differ from simple roof square footage.

Compatibility matters

Selection can differ by roof covering, slope, climate and manufacturer requirements. Do not select a product from price alone.

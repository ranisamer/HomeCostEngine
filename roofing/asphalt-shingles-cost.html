ROOFING MATERIAL COST

Asphalt shingle roof cost

Asphalt shingles are common, but two asphalt proposals can still be very different jobs. Compare the complete roof system—not just the shingle brand or a price per square foot.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

What you are actually buying

An asphalt roof replacement is a system. The visible shingles are only one line item. A complete proposal can include tear-off, deck repairs, underlayment, leak-barrier membrane where required or specified, starter course, field shingles, hip and ridge caps, flashing, drip edge, ventilation, fasteners, disposal, permits and cleanup.

Shingle grade is only one part of the price

Ask for the exact manufacturer and product line rather than a vague label such as “premium shingle.” Confirm matching starter and ridge products, underlayment type, flashing approach and ventilation work.

Use roof area, not house area

A 2,000-square-foot home does not necessarily have a 2,000-square-foot roof. Story count, footprint, garage, overhang and slope all matter. Use the Roofing Square Calculator after estimating the roof surface.

Plan for concealed deck repairs

Ask each contractor for a unit price or written allowance for plywood or OSB replacement so a change order is understandable rather than a surprise.

Compare quotes on the same scope

  1. Confirm measured roof area and square count.
  2. List the exact shingle and accessory system.
  3. State the tear-off layers included.
  4. Show how deck replacement is priced.
  5. Identify flashing replaced versus reused.
  6. Define ventilation changes.
  7. Separate permit, disposal and cleanup responsibilities.

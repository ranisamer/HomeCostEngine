ROOFING MATERIAL COST

Metal roof cost

Metal roofing cost depends as much on the system and detailing as on the sheet or panel itself. Identify the panel profile, fastening method, trim package and substrate before comparing bids.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

Start by defining the metal system

“Metal roof” can describe exposed-fastener panels, standing-seam systems and other profiles with very different installation requirements. Ask for panel manufacturer, profile, gauge or thickness specification, coating, seam or fastening method and warranty documents.

The trim package matters

Ridge, eave, rake, valley, wall, chimney, skylight and transition details can require fabricated or system-specific trim. Complex roofs contain more linear feet of these details and more labor-intensive cuts.

Substrate and tear-off change the job

Do not compare an overlay proposal with a tear-off-and-repair proposal as though they are the same scope.

Cost driverWhat to ask
Panel systemExposed fastener, standing seam or other profile?
AttachmentDirect fastened, clips, battens or another assembly?
Metal specificationMaterial, gauge/thickness and coating?
TrimWhich edge, valley, wall and penetration details?
SubstrateWhat deck preparation is assumed?

Geometry increases fabrication and labor

Valleys, dormers, intersecting roof planes and penetrations increase layout, cutting, flashing and trim work. Compare unit prices only when geometry is similar.

Use a complete-system quote

A useful metal proposal identifies panel system, underlayment, attachment method, trim, flashing, ventilation, tear-off, disposal, deck repair pricing and warranty.

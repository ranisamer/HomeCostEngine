ROOF EDGE DETAIL

Roof drip edge cost and scope

Drip edge is a small-looking roof component that touches several systems at once: roof covering, underlayment, fascia and often gutters.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

Ask whether drip edge is included

Some proposals list roof-edge metal explicitly; others bury it inside the system. Identify whether new metal is included at eaves and rakes and how it integrates with the selected roof covering.

Gutters can complicate the sequence

If gutters are being replaced, coordinate roof edge and gutter installation so one trade does not undo the other.

Fascia condition can change the job

Deteriorated fascia or deck edge can create repair work separate from the metal itself.

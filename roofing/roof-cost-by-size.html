ROOF SIZE PLANNING

Roof replacement cost by size

Size matters, but the right size is the roof surface—not simply the interior square footage of the house. Use footprint, pitch and measured roof planes first.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

House size and roof size are different

A one-story 2,000-square-foot home may have a larger roof footprint than a two-story home with the same living area. Garages, porches, overhangs and additions also change the surface.

Convert roof area to roofing squares

One roofing square represents 100 square feet of roof surface. A 2,240-square-foot measured roof is 22.4 squares before waste or ordering allowance.

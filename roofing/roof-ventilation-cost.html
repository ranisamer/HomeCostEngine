ATTIC & ROOF SYSTEM

Roof ventilation cost

Reroofing is a useful time to inspect ventilation assumptions. Intake and exhaust need to work as a system, and adding one vent type without understanding the attic can be a poor shortcut.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

Ventilation is a system question

Attic ventilation planning usually considers both intake and exhaust. Ridge vents, soffit vents, box vents and other approaches are not automatically interchangeable.

Why reroofing can change the scope

Roof replacement may expose blocked intake paths, damaged vents or an opportunity to change the exhaust strategy.

CheckDocument

Do not compare vent count alone

More vents are not automatically better. Compare design, location and manufacturer/local requirements.

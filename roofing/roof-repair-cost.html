ROOF REPAIR

Roof repair cost

A roof repair is priced around the defect and access—not the house size. Identify the failure, affected components and how much surrounding roofing must be opened and restored.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

Repair cost begins with diagnosis

A water stain does not necessarily sit directly below the roof defect. Water can travel along decking, rafters or penetrations.

Common repair categories

Repair typeScope question
Damaged coveringHow much surrounding material must be removed?
Pipe/vent flashingIs the flashing or surrounding roof damaged?
Wall/chimney flashingCan the detail be rebuilt locally?
Valley leakWhat layers must be opened and restored?
Deck damageHow much sheathing or framing is affected?

Small repairs still have setup cost

Travel, access, diagnostic time, materials and warranty responsibility mean tiny repairs do not always scale neatly by square foot.

When to evaluate replacement

If repairs recur, multiple areas are failing or the roof is near the end of its service life, compare with the repair-versus-replacement guide.

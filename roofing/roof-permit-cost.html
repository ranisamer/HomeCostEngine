Roof permit cost

Roof permit fees and inspection steps are local. Identify the authority, permit responsibility and inspection sequence before signing a contract.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

There is no reliable national permit fee

Permit schedules are set by local jurisdictions and can depend on project value, roof type, structural work, wind or fire zones and other rules. Treat any national fee as a placeholder until the property’s authority having jurisdiction is verified.

Ask who pulls the permit

The contract should state whether the contractor or owner is responsible for the permit, fees, notices and inspection scheduling.

VerifyWhy
JurisdictionCity, county or another authority may control the permit.
Permit triggerRepair and full replacement may be treated differently.
Fee basisFlat fee, valuation, area or another schedule.
InspectionsSome projects require more than one step.

Separate permit fees from code upgrades

A permit fee is not the same thing as the cost of code-required work. Deck fastening, flashing, ventilation or wind mitigation can be separate scope.

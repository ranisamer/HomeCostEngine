Roof inspection cost and scope

The value of a roof inspection depends on what is inspected and documented. Define the scope before comparing inspection fees or relying on a quick visual check.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

Different inspections answer different questions

A pre-purchase inspection, leak investigation, storm assessment and pre-replacement survey are not necessarily the same service. Ask what will be accessed and documented.

AreaWhat may be reviewed
Roof coveringWear, damage or displacement signs
FlashingsWalls, chimneys, valleys and penetrations
DrainageGutters and roof drainage path
AtticLeak signs, ventilation and visible deck
InteriorStains or moisture clues

Access changes the inspection

Steepness, height, weather, fragile materials and safety conditions can limit direct roof walking. Ask what method will be used and what limitations will be stated.

Use findings to improve quote requests

A documented list of suspect flashing, deck areas, ventilation or drainage problems helps contractors price the same scope.

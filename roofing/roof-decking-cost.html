Roof decking replacement cost

Decking is the structural surface beneath the roof covering. Because damage is often confirmed only after tear-off, use a written allowance or unit price instead of pretending the quantity is known.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

Why decking becomes a change-order issue

Soft, delaminated or deteriorated sheathing may be partly hidden by the existing roof. The complete condition may not be visible until the covering and underlayment are removed.

Price the unknown before work starts

Ask for a unit price for common sheathing repair or a written allowance that explains the assumed quantity.

ROOF REMOVAL

Roof tear-off cost

Tear-off is more than removing old shingles. Layer count, material weight, access, site protection, loading and disposal can change the removal portion of a roofing quote.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

Count layers before comparing bids

A proposal that assumes one layer is not equivalent to a proposal that includes two or more layers. Ask what happens if another layer is discovered.

Material type changes handling

Asphalt shingles, tile, wood products and metal do not have the same weight, fastening or disposal profile.

Access is a real cost driver

A dumpster beside a one-story roof is a different job from a steep multi-story house with restricted driveway access. Long carry distances and protection of pools, glass or landscaping add work.

AskWhy
How many layers are included?Extra layers add removal and disposal.
What material is being removed?Weight and handling vary.
Where will debris be staged?Access affects labor and protection.
Are dump/container fees included?Avoids an omitted disposal line.

Tear-off exposes deck condition

Keep a separate deck repair allowance so concealed repairs do not disappear inside the removal line.

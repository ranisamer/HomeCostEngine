ROOFING MATERIAL COST

Tile roof cost

Tile roofing is heavy, detail-sensitive and often long-lived. A useful budget separates the tile surface from underlayment, flashing, structure, attachment and access.

Estimate your roof →Roofing cost library

The tile is not the whole roof

Tile may remain visually serviceable while underlayment or flashing reaches the end of its useful condition. A reroof can involve lifting and resetting reusable tile, installing new tile, replacing underlayment, repairing deck areas and rebuilding flashing details.

Verify structural assumptions

If a project changes from a lighter roof to tile, structural suitability should be verified by the appropriate local professional rather than assumed from an online estimate.

Access and handling affect labor

Tile is heavier and more fragile to move than many sheet or shingle products. Multi-story access, staging distance and protection of finished surfaces can add handling time.

Compare underlayment, not just tile brand

Ask what is underneath the visible tile and how penetrations and transitions are detailed.

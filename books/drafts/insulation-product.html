Why use it?

Make zone boundaries and evidence comparable before treating two totals as equivalent. The original worksheets link product and installed-result claims to removal scope, minimum charges and completion records.

For U.S. homeowners comparing actual professionally assessed insulation offers. Skip this if you need an installation procedure, product selection, code target or promised savings.

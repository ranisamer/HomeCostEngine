How the state adjustment works

HomeCostEngine starts with a national installed-cost planning range and uses the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis 2024 Regional Price Parity as a broad state price-level signal. The model applies 65% of a state’s deviation from the national baseline because general consumer price levels do not translate one-for-one into construction pricing.

What the state number does not know

A statewide index cannot see your roof pitch, number of layers, deck condition, access, permit jurisdiction, exact product, ventilation design, insurance scope or contractor availability. The state range is a screening benchmark—not a local bid.

Use the interactive planner

The state-adjusted renovation planner covers every state and lets you change project size, finish level, market type, home age, complexity, demolition and contingency. For roof-specific geometry and material options, use the roof replacement calculator.

Method note: Prices are planning estimates in U.S. dollars. Always verify measurements, code requirements, permits and scope with qualified local professionals.

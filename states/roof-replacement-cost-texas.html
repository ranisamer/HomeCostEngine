Texas roofing costs depend heavily on where the house is

Dallas–Fort Worth, Austin, Houston, San Antonio, the Gulf Coast and smaller Texas markets do not behave like one labor market. Hail exposure, wind, roof complexity, access and contractor demand can matter more than the statewide price-level adjustment shown above.

Coastal windstorm rules deserve special attention

The Texas Department of Insurance operates a windstorm program for designated catastrophe areas. For applicable work, TDI states that windstorm certification applications filed from April 1, 2026 use the 2024 IRC or IBC standards adopted by the program. That is a specific program requirement—not a statement that every Texas roof follows the same permitting path.

Questions that make a Texas quote easier to compare

QuestionWhy ask it
What exact shingle, metal or tile product is included?Generic material labels hide rating, warranty and price differences.
What is the measured roof area?House floor area is not roof surface area.
How is deck repair priced?Storm damage and prior leaks may not be fully visible before tear-off.
Is this property in a TDI windstorm-designated area?Coastal certification requirements can affect documentation and inspection.
What ventilation work is included?Replacing shingles does not automatically correct attic-airflow problems.

Hail resistance should be discussed precisely

If impact performance matters to you, ask for the exact product and test/rating the contractor is relying on. A marketing phrase such as “hail resistant” should not replace a model number and manufacturer documentation. No roof covering can guarantee zero storm damage.

Insurance work and contractor scope are different documents

A claim estimate may be useful for coverage discussions, but your construction contract should still specify products, quantities, tear-off, flashing, ventilation, cleanup and change-order terms. Do not let the insurance paperwork become the only description of the job.

Texas roof FAQ

Is a 2,000 sq ft house a 2,000 sq ft roof?

No. Story count, footprint, overhangs, garages and pitch change roof surface area.

Does the TDI windstorm program apply everywhere in Texas?

No. It applies to designated catastrophe areas. Check the property and current program requirements directly with TDI.

Should I choose the bid closest to the state midpoint?

No. Compare complete scope, products, exclusions, credentials and warranty. A midpoint is only a planning reference.

## Stage 2: research and publish distinct gaps

Before assigning a URL, inspect all existing guides, calculators and book material. Choose one canonical owner of each task. The following are research hypotheses only, not validated search demand or approved publication quotas:

| Cluster | Candidate homeowner task | Existing cluster to inspect before making a page |
|---|---|---|
| Roofing | Audit a storm-damage estimate against repair scope and exclusions | Repair versus replacement, contractor and quote-comparison guides |
| Roofing | Separate roof warranty coverage from installation and maintenance responsibilities | Existing material, flashing and contractor guides |
| Concrete | Compare demolition-and-replacement bids including haul-off | Concrete driveway and delivered-cost guides |
| Concrete | Compare ready-mix delivery, pumping and access alternatives | Ready-mix-versus-bags and delivered-cost articles |
| HVAC | Compare electrical-service exclusions in replacement bids | Heat-pump installation and HVAC estimate guides |
| HVAC | Check whether duct leakage testing and sealing are included | Ductwork and repair-versus-replacement pages |
| Flooring | Plan furniture moving, removal, transitions and disposal scope | Complete flooring cost, prep and material comparison guides |
| Plumbing | Compare storage-tank removal, drain-pan and connection scope | Water-heater sizing and replacement pages |
| Windows | Compare pocket and full-frame replacement scope | Existing window cost and quote guides |
| Outdoor work | Separate drainage correction from a surface refresh | Gravel quantity, driveway cost and concrete guides |

If an existing page already serves the task, improve that page rather than generating another URL. Reject pages distinguished only by year, state, city or house size. No copied supplier descriptions, fabricated prices, fake reviews or invented local expertise.

## Stage 3: milestone gates

- First checkpoint: the existing weak-answer backlog is reviewed and each retained page has a defensible distinct task.
- Expand toward 200, then 250 useful sitemap URLs in reviewed batches. These are planning checkpoints, not deadlines or a requirement to fill slots.
- At every checkpoint, inspect actual completed U.S. clicks, query-to-page relevance, comparable-position CTR and available authorized tool/download reporting. Compare equivalent periods only when history supports it.
- Review 500 and 1,000 only after the smaller library produces useful outcomes and there is a defensible independent intent inventory. A page-count milestone alone does not justify expansion.

## Publication gate for every new page

A new original article requires a distinct uncovered intent, 900+ substantive words when appropriate to the task, an original or lawfully reusable unique relevant raster hero with source/credit, descriptive alt and dimensions, reviewed date/byline, verified primary sources, correct examples, canonical and matching structured data. Existing updates need useful substantive depth without padding. Test any changed calculator. Add natural in-body and contextual inbound links and verify the live output.

Link to the existing free workbook only when it supports the task. Keep optional marketing consent unchecked and preserve unsubscribes. Brevo timed marketing and external distribution remain inactive pending account access and owner-authorized delivery/unsubscribe tests. Paid acquisition remains on hold until genuine tool use, workbook delivery and the complete paid-book delivery journey are verified and a separate budget is authorized.

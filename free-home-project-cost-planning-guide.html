What this guide does—and does not do

The guide teaches a budgeting process. It does not provide a contractor quote or guarantee a final project cost. Local labor, product availability, access, condition, permit rules and design decisions can change the result. Every numerical example in the PDF is identified as hypothetical.

For older homes, code-regulated work or safety-sensitive projects, use appropriately qualified professionals and verify requirements with your local authority. The source section links to homeowner guidance from the Federal Trade Commission, U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, U.S. Department of Energy and HUD.

Who it is for

It is written for U.S. homeowners preparing for roofing, HVAC, concrete, painting, flooring, kitchen, bathroom or general remodeling work. You can also reuse the worksheets for smaller repairs when you want written scope and price clarity.

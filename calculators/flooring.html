Flooring cost per square foot

Flooring cost per square foot usually combines the material price with installation labor, then adds project-specific items such as waste, removal, delivery and floor preparation. Enter your own material and labor assumptions above to compare scenarios.

How this flooring cost calculator works

The calculator adds your chosen waste allowance to the measured floor area and prices both material and labor over the purchase area. This makes it easier to compare quotes using the same square-foot assumptions.

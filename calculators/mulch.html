Mulch volume made simple

Area × depth produces cubic volume; the tool converts that result into cubic yards. Bulk suppliers typically sell by the cubic yard, while bagged mulch is sold by cubic feet.

Ordering tip

Irregular beds, slopes and settling make exact volume difficult. Round up sensibly and confirm delivery minimums and fees with the supplier.

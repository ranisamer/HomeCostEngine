Roof tear-off cost per square in 2026

Roofers often discuss removal in roofing squares, where one square equals 100 square feet of roof surface. A published removal range of $1–$3 per square foot is equivalent to about $100–$300 per square before project-specific extras. Heavier systems such as tile or slate can be higher.

Asphalt shingles / metal$1–$3 / sq ft$100–$300 / square
Tile / slate$2–$5+ / sq ft$200–$500+ / square

Why two roof layers cost more to remove

Extra layers increase labor, fastener removal, debris weight and disposal. The calculator applies a planning factor for second and third layers so a one-layer quote is not compared directly with a multi-layer scope. A contractor may price extra layers differently, so replace this assumption with a written unit rate when you have one.

Do not calculate from house square footage

A 2,000 sq ft house is not automatically a 2,000 sq ft roof. Story count, garage area, overhangs and pitch change roof surface area. Use the Roof Pitch Calculator or Roofing Square Calculator if roof quantity is still uncertain.

What should a tear-off quote include?

  • Number and type of existing roof layers included.
  • Removal labor and ordinary site protection.
  • Debris loading, hauling and disposal or a clearly separated allowance.
  • How hidden deck damage will be documented and priced after removal.
  • Any surcharge for steep pitch, height, difficult access or special material handling.

Example: 2,000 sq ft asphalt roof

For a 2,000 sq ft roof with one asphalt layer, the broad $1–$3 per sq ft reference equals about $2,000–$6,000 for removal before adding a separate disposal allowance. The wide range is intentional: this tool is designed to show what assumptions should be replaced with local written pricing.

Sources and methodology

Removal benchmarks are cross-checked against HomeGuide roofing material costs and HomeGuide roofing labor costs. Safety and scope questions should also be reviewed with a qualified roofing contractor.

Planning limit: This calculator is for budgeting and quote comparison. Site conditions, local labor, code, permit requirements, safety setup and contractor minimum charges can materially change the final price.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to tear off a roof per square?

A broad 2026 planning reference is about $100–$300 per roofing square for shingles or metal before project-specific factors, while heavier tile or slate removal can run higher. One roofing square equals 100 square feet.

How much more does a second roof layer cost to remove?

A second layer increases labor and disposal, but there is no universal multiplier. This calculator uses a planning adjustment so the extra layer is visible; replace it with your contractor’s written additional-layer rate when available.

Does roof tear-off cost include disposal?

Sometimes. Quotes may bundle removal and disposal or list dumpsters and dump fees separately. The calculator keeps disposal visible so you can avoid counting it twice.

Should I use house square footage or roof square footage?

Use actual roof surface area. Living area can differ significantly from roof area because of pitch, garages, porches, overhangs and story count.

Is tear-off normally included in a roof replacement quote?

Often, but the scope varies. Verify the included number of layers, material type, disposal, site protection and how concealed decking repairs will be handled.

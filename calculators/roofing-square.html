How this calculator works

The calculator separates the measured roof quantity from the planning allowance so you can see both numbers. It then multiplies the adjusted roofing squares by the bundle count you enter.

InputWhat it meansWhy it matters
Roof areaActual roof surface in square feetHouse living area is not a substitute
Waste allowanceExtra percentage for cuts and layoutComplex roofs may need a different allowance
Bundles per squarePackage count for the exact shingle productCoverage is product-specific

Formula used

Base squares = roof area ÷ 100. Adjusted squares = base squares × waste factor. Approximate bundles = adjusted squares × bundles per square.

Worked example

For a measured 2,000-square-foot roof with a 10% planning allowance, the calculator returns 20 base squares and 22 adjusted squares. If the exact product uses 3 bundles per square, the planning quantity is approximately 66 bundles.

Important: Confirm package coverage on the manufacturer's current product documentation. Starter, ridge/hip cap and other accessories may be packaged separately and should not be assumed to be included in the field-shingle bundle count.

Do not use house square footage automatically

A home's floor area and roof area can differ substantially. Story count, pitch, overhangs, garages, porches and roof geometry all affect the roof surface. If you only know footprint and slope, use the Roof Pitch Calculator before converting to squares.

Choosing a waste allowance

Waste is not a universal fixed percentage. A simple gable roof with clear dimensions can differ from a roof with many valleys, hips, dormers and penetrations. Use the calculator to test scenarios, then verify the final order quantity with the installer or supplier.

What this calculator does not include

  • Starter strip quantity
  • Ridge and hip cap quantity
  • Underlayment rolls
  • Ice/water protection where required
  • Flashing and edge metal
  • Fasteners
  • Ventilation components

FAQ

What is one roofing square?

One roofing square equals 100 square feet of roof surface.

Why can the bundle count differ by product?

Packaging and stated coverage differ. Use the current manufacturer's coverage information for the exact product.

Should I round up?

Material is purchased in whole packages, but the final practical order should account for product packaging, roof layout, accessories and supplier return policies.

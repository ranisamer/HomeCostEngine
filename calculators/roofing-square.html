What is a roofing square?

One roofing square equals 100 square feet of roof surface. Contractors and suppliers commonly use squares when discussing roofing quantity.

Why add waste?

Valleys, hips, ridges, starter strips and cuts create waste. Complex roofs can require a larger allowance than simple rectangular roof planes.

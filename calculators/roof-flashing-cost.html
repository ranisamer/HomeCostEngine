Roof flashing cost per linear foot

Some flashing is priced by linear foot, especially edge or continuous metal, while other details are priced by location or assembly. HomeGuide publishes drip-edge replacement around $1.50–$9 per linear foot, but that does not mean chimney, step or skylight flashing should be priced with the same unit.

Repair vs complete flashing replacement

A small reseal or local repair should not be compared directly with removal and reconstruction of a full chimney or wall-flashing assembly. HomeGuide’s broad 2026 data places many minor repairs around $200–$500, while larger or complete replacement work can reach $500–$1,500 or more depending on location and complexity.

What should the roofing proposal say?

  • Which existing flashing will be reused and which will be replaced.
  • Material and finish for new metal where specified.
  • Whether adjacent shingles or siding are included.
  • How chimney counterflashing or masonry work is handled.
  • Whether skylight kits and vent boots are new.
  • Any separate allowances for concealed deck damage around penetrations.

Do not hide flashing inside “miscellaneous”

Flashing is a small percentage of roof area but a critical water-management detail. Keeping the quantities visible helps compare contractor scopes and makes it easier to understand why two bids with similar shingle prices may have different totals.

Sources and methodology

Cost references are based on HomeGuide’s 2026 roof flashing repair guide and its broader roof repair cost guide. Use exact local scope and unit prices for decisions.

Planning limit: This calculator is for budgeting and quote comparison. Site conditions, local labor, code, permit requirements, safety setup and contractor minimum charges can materially change the final price.

Frequently asked questions

How much does roof flashing cost per linear foot?

It depends on the flashing type. HomeGuide publishes drip-edge replacement around $1.50 to $9 per linear foot, but chimney, wall, valley and skylight details are often priced by assembly rather than one universal linear-foot rate.

How much does chimney flashing cost?

Published 2026 consumer guides show a wide range because resealing, partial repair and full replacement are different jobs. Use a per-chimney allowance from a written local quote in the calculator.

How much does it cost to replace flashing around a roof vent?

Minor vent flashing repairs are often a few hundred dollars, but access, roof type and surrounding shingle work matter. Enter the contractor’s per-boot or per-penetration price when available.

Should flashing be replaced during a roof replacement?

Some flashing may be reusable and some may need replacement. The contractor should identify the approach for walls, valleys, chimneys, skylights and penetrations rather than using one vague statement.

Is drip edge the same as roof flashing?

Drip edge is one type of edge flashing. Roof flashing also includes details at walls, valleys, chimneys, skylights and penetrations.

Concrete volume formula

Volume is calculated from length × width × thickness, converted from cubic feet to cubic yards, then increased by your selected waste allowance. One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet.

Why add waste?

Spillage, uneven subgrade, form variation and ordering increments can leave a project short. A modest waste allowance creates breathing room, but large or structural pours should be verified by the supplier or contractor.

Roof decking replacement cost per sheet in 2026

Roof decking—also called roof sheathing—is the structural panel or board layer attached to rafters or trusses beneath the roofing system. When a reroof exposes damaged decking, contractors commonly price the added work by the sheet, by the square foot, or under a time-and-material change order.

Two current national consumer cost references, Angi and HomeGuide, publish installed roof sheathing ranges around $2–$5 per square foot. Multiplying that by 32 sq ft in a standard 4×8 panel produces a rough equivalent of $64–$160 per installed sheet. Use that only as a screening benchmark because actual proposals can fall outside it.

What “cost per sheet” should include

A per-sheet number is only useful when the scope behind it is clear. Ask whether the installed rate includes the replacement panel, removal of damaged sheathing, ordinary cutting, fastening, disposal, crew labor and any underlayment patching needed as the roof system is restored. A lower rate that excludes several of those items may not be cheaper once the change order is complete.

Also separate roof decking from structural framing. Damaged rafters or trusses, fascia, soffit, interior ceiling repair, insulation, mold assessment or electrical work are different scopes and should not be silently buried in a sheathing unit price.

How many sheets of roof decking do I need?

A standard 4×8 panel has a nominal area of 32 square feet. For a simple planning estimate:

Estimated sheets = replacement area × allowance ÷ 32, rounded up to whole sheets.

For example, 192 sq ft of documented replacement area is exactly six 4×8 panels before any allowance. A 5% planning allowance produces 201.6 sq ft, so the calculator rounds the result to seven full panels. Real panel layout can differ because sheathing edges need proper framing support and damaged areas do not always align neatly with full-sheet boundaries.

How many sheets for a 2,000 sq ft roof?

If 2,000 sq ft is the actual roof surface area and the entire deck is being replaced, dividing 2,000 by 32 gives 62.5 panels, so the theoretical minimum is 63 full 4×8 sheets before cuts, layout and any justified allowance. At 5%, the calculator estimates about 66 sheets.

A 2,000 sq ft house is not automatically a 2,000 sq ft roof. Story count, pitch, overhangs, garages and porches can change roof surface area substantially. Use the Roofing Square Calculator and Roof Pitch Calculator if you are still establishing roof quantity.

OSB vs plywood roof decking cost

OSB and plywood are both common structural panel categories, but the final choice should be based on the specified product, thickness, span rating, framing spacing, roofing system and locally adopted requirements. Material-only pricing can differ considerably from installed replacement pricing because labor and access can dominate small repairs.

Do not automatically choose the cheapest panel or assume plywood is always required. Ask the contractor to write the exact panel specification into the proposal and explain why it matches the existing roof assembly and local requirements.

Cost to replace plywood on a roof

Searches for “cost to replace plywood on roof,” “roof sheathing replacement cost,” and “roof decking replacement cost” usually describe the same budget problem: part or all of the structural roof deck must be removed and replaced. The cost is driven less by the wording and more by the quantity, material specification, roof access and whether replacement happens during an already-open reroof or as a standalone repair.

A few panels replaced while the roof covering is already removed can have a very different effective unit cost from an emergency isolated repair that requires opening and rebuilding finished roofing around one damaged area.

Why decking is often a surprise during roof replacement

Shingles and underlayment conceal the top of the deck before tear-off. An attic inspection can sometimes show staining, sagging or visible deterioration, but insulation and finished spaces can block the view. This is why the final number of damaged panels often cannot be confirmed until the old roof covering is removed.

The uncertainty is normal; an undefined billing method is avoidable. Before signing, ask the roofer for a written installed price per additional sheet, what the rate includes, how damage will be photographed or documented, and who can authorize extra work.

Roof decking labor cost

Labor is affected by more than the act of fastening a panel. Crews may need to remove deteriorated material, cut new panels around penetrations, move material across a steep roof, stage debris safely and coordinate decking replacement with underlayment and weather protection. Height, pitch, valleys, dormers and limited access can increase time.

For this reason, HomeCostEngine does not hide a universal labor number inside the calculator. Enter the contractor's installed per-sheet rate when you have it. If you do not, the broad 2026 reference band helps you test whether your preliminary allowance is in a plausible national range before getting local quotes.

What makes roof sheathing replacement more expensive?

  • Steeper pitch or greater height: handling and fall-protection requirements can increase labor.
  • Small scattered repairs: several isolated panels can take more cutting and movement than one continuous area.
  • Complex geometry: valleys, dormers, skylights, chimneys and roof transitions increase cuts.
  • Heavier or different panel specification: thickness and product type change material cost and handling.
  • Standalone access: repairing decking beneath an otherwise finished roof requires opening and restoring the roofing system.
  • Disposal and site constraints: limited driveway access or sensitive landscaping can slow staging and cleanup.
  • Structural damage: rafter or truss repair is a different scope and can materially increase cost.

What to put in the roofing contract before tear-off

  1. Panel type, thickness or specification expected for replacement.
  2. Installed price per sheet or another clearly defined pricing method.
  3. Whether any number of replacement sheets is included in the base quote.
  4. What the unit price includes: removal, panel, cutting, fastening and disposal.
  5. How additional damaged decking will be documented.
  6. Who has authority to approve additional work and by what method.
  7. How structural framing or other non-decking damage will be priced separately.

Decking cost vs total roof replacement cost

Roof decking is one line item inside a larger roofing system. A full replacement budget can also include tear-off, disposal, underlayment, finished roofing, starter, ridge or hip cap, flashing, ventilation, edge metal, permits and contingency. Use the Roof Replacement Cost Calculator for the larger project, then keep decking as a transparent separate allowance so hidden damage does not disappear inside one unexplained total.

Roof decking replacement cost FAQ

How much does roof decking replacement cost per sheet?

As a broad 2026 national reference, current published installed ranges around $2–$5 per sq ft translate to roughly $64–$160 for a standard 32-sq-ft 4×8 panel. Local proposals can be outside that range.

How much does roof decking cost per square foot?

Current national consumer cost guides commonly cite roughly $2–$5 per sq ft installed. The useful number for your project is the written local rate tied to a defined scope.

How many square feet does one sheet of plywood or OSB cover?

A standard 4×8 panel has 32 sq ft of nominal area before cuts.

How many sheets of plywood for a 2,000 sq ft roof?

If 2,000 sq ft is actual roof surface and the entire area is being re-decked, the theoretical minimum is 62.5 sheets, which rounds to 63 whole 4×8 panels before layout and waste. At a 5% allowance, this calculator estimates about 66 sheets.

Is roof decking the same as roof sheathing?

In residential roofing discussions, the terms are often used interchangeably for the structural board or panel layer attached to roof framing beneath the roof covering.

Is OSB cheaper than plywood for roof decking?

OSB often has a lower material price, but installed cost also includes labor, access and replacement scope. The correct material and thickness depend on the roof design, framing, span rating and local requirements.

Does insurance pay for roof decking replacement?

Coverage depends on the policy, cause of loss, exclusions and claim facts. Ask the insurer or licensed insurance professional about the specific policy rather than assuming decking is automatically covered.

Should every roof replacement include new decking?

No. Whether decking must be replaced depends on its condition and the requirements of the roof assembly. Sound decking may remain; damaged or unsuitable sections may require replacement.

How this HVAC replacement cost calculator works

This tool uses home size as a rough starting point for system capacity and a cost range by system type. Correct equipment sizing requires a professional load calculation that considers climate, insulation, windows, air leakage, orientation and ductwork.

HVAC replacement cost by home size

Home size is one of the most common starting points for HVAC budgeting, but it is not a substitute for professional sizing. Compare our guides for HVAC replacement cost for a 1,500 sq ft house and HVAC replacement cost for a 2,000 sq ft house.

Heat pump installation cost vs central air

If you are comparing system types, read heat pump installation cost factors and our heat pump vs central air cost comparison.

Repair or replace your HVAC system?

Age, repair frequency, efficiency, comfort problems and the scope of the next repair all matter. Use our HVAC repair vs replacement guide before committing to a full system replacement.

Before replacing a system

Ask for model numbers, efficiency ratings, labor, controls, ductwork scope, permits, disposal and warranty terms on the written proposal. Compare like-for-like scopes rather than total price alone.

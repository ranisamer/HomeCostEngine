Roof pitch explained

Roof pitch describes vertical rise compared with horizontal run. A 6:12 roof rises 6 inches for every 12 inches of horizontal run.

Why pitch matters

Pitch affects roof surface area, walkability, labor difficulty and sometimes material suitability. The area multiplier can help convert horizontal footprint area into approximate sloped roof area for simple roof planes.

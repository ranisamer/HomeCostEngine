How this roof estimate works

The calculator starts with an installed cost-per-square-foot planning range for the selected roofing material, then adjusts for pitch/complexity, building height, local market level and tear-off. It is intentionally transparent rather than pretending to know a contractor’s exact bid.

What can move a real quote?

Decking repairs, flashing, ventilation, underlayment, disposal, permits, access, warranty, product brand and local labor can all change the final price. A low quote may also exclude items that a higher quote includes.

Best way to use the number

Use the range to establish a budget, then compare at least three itemized quotes with the same scope. Ask each contractor to identify exclusions and allowances in writing.

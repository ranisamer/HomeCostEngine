How this roof replacement cost calculator works

The calculator starts with an installed cost-per-square-foot planning range for the selected material. It adjusts that range for pitch and complexity, building height, broad state price levels and local-market sensitivity. It then adds tear-off layers, decking-repair allowance, skylights or other complex roof details, permits and owner-controlled contingency.

State methodology: State selection uses 2024 Regional Price Parities from the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis. HomeCostEngine moderates the difference from the national baseline because construction-specific labor, products, insurance, permits and contractor availability do not move exactly with general consumer prices.

What can move a real roofing quote?

Decking repairs, flashing, ventilation, underlayment, disposal, permits, access, warranty, product brand and local labor can all change the final price. A low quote may also exclude items that a higher quote includes.

Compare tear-off, decking and roof details

Ask every bidder to state the number of layers being removed, the unit price for unexpected deck replacement, and how chimneys, skylights, valleys and wall transitions are flashed. These scope differences can explain a large price gap between quotes.

Compare roofing materials and measurements

Use the Roofing Square Calculator to convert roof area into roofing squares, the Roof Pitch Calculator to understand slope, and read metal roof vs shingles cost before comparing material options.

Best way to use the number

Use the range to establish a budget, then compare at least three itemized quotes with the same scope. Ask each contractor to identify exclusions and allowances in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Is house square footage the same as roof area?

No. Roof pitch, overhangs, attached garages, porches and the number of stories change actual roof surface area. Use measured roof area when available.

Does the estimate include tear-off and permits?

Yes, based on the tear-off layers and permit allowance you enter. Disposal, access and local requirements still need confirmation in contractor proposals.

How should I use the decking repair allowance?

Enter a reasonable square-foot allowance for planning, then ask contractors for a written unit price and approval process for deck repairs discovered after tear-off.

Is the state adjustment an exact local quote?

No. It is a broad state price-level sensitivity. Metro conditions, storms, contractor availability, product choice, insurance and code requirements can move local pricing.

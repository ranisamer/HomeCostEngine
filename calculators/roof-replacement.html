How this roof replacement cost calculator works

The calculator starts with an installed roofing cost-per-square-foot planning range for the selected material, then adjusts for pitch and complexity, building height, local market level and tear-off. It is designed for budgeting rather than predicting an exact contractor bid.

Roof replacement cost per square foot

Roof replacement cost per square foot changes with roofing material, labor, roof access, pitch, tear-off, decking repairs and local pricing. Enter the actual roof surface area above instead of relying only on your home’s interior square footage.

Planning a common house-size scenario? See our guides for roof replacement cost for a 1,500 sq ft house, roof replacement cost for a 2,000 sq ft house, and roof replacement cost for a 2,500 sq ft house.

What can move a real roofing quote?

Decking repairs, flashing, ventilation, underlayment, disposal, permits, access, warranty, product brand and local labor can all change the final price. A low quote may also exclude items that a higher quote includes.

Compare roofing materials and measurements

Use the Roofing Square Calculator to convert roof area into roofing squares, the Roof Pitch Calculator to understand slope, and read metal roof vs shingles cost before comparing material options.

Best way to use the number

Use the range to establish a budget, then compare at least three itemized quotes with the same scope. Ask each contractor to identify exclusions and allowances in writing.

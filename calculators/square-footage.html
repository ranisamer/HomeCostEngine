How to calculate square footage

For a rectangle, multiply length by width. For a square, multiply one side by itself. For a circle, use π × radius². The calculator also converts the result to square yards and acres.

For irregular spaces

Break the area into simple rectangles or other basic shapes, calculate each section separately, then add the results. This is usually more reliable than estimating one complex outline.

How this calculator works

The calculator converts simple shape dimensions into square feet, square yards and acres. Use it for measured project areas such as rooms, patios, landscape beds and flooring zones.

Formulas used

ShapeFormula
RectangleLength × width
SquareSide × side
Circleπ × (diameter ÷ 2)²

For irregular spaces

Break the project into simple shapes, calculate each section separately and add them together. For an L-shaped room, two rectangles are usually easier to measure and audit than one rough outline.

Measured area versus purchase quantity

Square footage tells you the geometric area. Flooring, tile, paint and landscape materials may need project-specific allowances for cuts, pattern matching, coats, overlap or waste. Keep measured area separate from the allowance so you can see how the purchase quantity was built.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing inches and feet without converting
  • Using house floor area as roof area
  • Ignoring closets or alcoves that receive the material
  • Subtracting openings twice
  • Adding waste before confirming measured area

Where to use the result next

Send the measured area to the Flooring Calculator, Paint Calculator, Mulch Calculator or Cubic Yard Calculator, depending on the project.

FAQ

How many square feet are in one square yard?

9 square feet.

How many square feet are in one acre?

43,560 square feet.

Should I round the measurement?

Keep the measured area as precise as practical, then round the final purchase quantity based on package sizes or supplier increments.

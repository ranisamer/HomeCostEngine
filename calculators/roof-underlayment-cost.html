Roof underlayment cost per square foot

Underlayment sits between the roof deck and the finished roof covering. In 2026, Angi publishes a broad replacement range of about $0.60–$2.70 per square foot in most cases. That range includes projects with different product types, roof pitches and local labor markets, so the calculator lets you replace generic assumptions with your own roll price and labor rate.

How many rolls of roof underlayment do I need?

Use roof surface area × overlap/waste allowance ÷ effective roll coverage, then round up to whole rolls. Do not use advertised nominal coverage if the product’s required side laps and end laps reduce effective coverage.

Example: 2,000 sq ft × 10% allowance = 2,200 sq ft to cover. At 1,000 effective sq ft per roll, the order quantity rounds up to 3 rolls.

Felt vs synthetic vs self-adhered membrane

CategoryWhy the cost can differ
Asphalt-saturated feltLower material cost, heavier rolls and product-specific coverage.
Synthetic underlaymentOften larger coverage per roll and different fastening / exposure requirements.
Self-adhered leak barrierHigher material cost and typically used in specified zones or systems rather than priced like field underlayment.

Underlayment is not always one roof-wide line item

Valleys, eaves, penetrations and other vulnerable locations may require a different membrane or detail. Ask the roofer to identify the main field underlayment and any separate leak-barrier material so two quotes can be compared on equal scope.

What to verify in a contractor quote

  • Manufacturer and exact product.
  • Coverage and number of rolls included.
  • Leak-barrier locations and widths.
  • Whether old underlayment removal is part of tear-off.
  • Deck preparation, fasteners and flashing integration.
  • Any material or labor allowance that can change after tear-off.

Sources and methodology

The 2026 benchmark is based on Angi’s roof underlayment replacement cost guide. HomeGuide also publishes broader installed underlayment ranges in its roofing material pricing guide.

Planning limit: This calculator is for budgeting and quote comparison. Site conditions, local labor, code, permit requirements, safety setup and contractor minimum charges can materially change the final price.

Frequently asked questions

How much does roof underlayment cost per square foot?

Angi publishes a broad 2026 replacement range around $0.60 to $2.70 per square foot in most cases. Product type, roof pitch, local labor and whether the work is part of a full reroof can change the final price.

How many rolls of underlayment do I need for a 2,000 sq ft roof?

It depends on effective coverage per roll and required overlap. For example, 2,000 sq ft with a 10% allowance is 2,200 sq ft. At 1,000 effective sq ft per roll, you would round up to 3 rolls.

Is synthetic underlayment more expensive than felt?

Material pricing often differs, but total installed cost also depends on roll coverage, fastening, labor and the roof system. Compare the exact products rather than material names alone.

Does a roof replacement include new underlayment?

Many full replacement proposals include new underlayment, but the product and detail zones can differ. Ask for the exact product and scope in writing.

Should I add waste to roof underlayment?

Allow for required laps, cuts and detail areas. The correct allowance depends on the product instructions and roof geometry, so use the manufacturer’s effective coverage when possible.

How many gallons of paint do you need?

The calculator estimates four-wall area, subtracts a simple allowance for doors and windows, multiplies by the number of coats, then divides by your paint’s stated coverage.

Remember primer and trim

Primer, ceilings, doors, baseboards and trim are not included in this wall-paint result. Textured or porous surfaces may also use more paint than the can’s nominal coverage.

How much paint do I need for a room?

The calculator estimates four-wall area, subtracts a simple allowance for doors and windows, multiplies by the number of coats, then divides by your paint’s stated coverage. Use the manufacturer’s coverage figure when available.

Paint coverage per gallon

Coverage depends on product, color change, surface texture and porosity. Textured or porous surfaces may need more paint than the can’s nominal coverage, while primer may be needed separately.

Cost to paint a room

The calculator above estimates paint quantity and material cost, not a full professional painting quote. For labor and project-cost planning, see our cost to paint a room guide.

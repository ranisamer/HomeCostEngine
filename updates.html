How we decide what to update

Priority goes to pages with thin explanations, unclear assumptions, stale references, broken links, duplicated wording or calculations that need more context. A date changes only when a substantive review or edit is completed.

What a review checks

  • Formula and unit consistency
  • Whether assumptions and exclusions are visible
  • Whether examples are clearly labeled as examples
  • Whether external references are credible and relevant
  • Whether the page answers a real homeowner planning question
  • Whether internal links lead to the next useful tool or guide
  • Whether safety, permit or professional boundaries are clear

Report an issue

If you find a calculation problem, outdated link or unclear statement, send the page URL and details through the contact page. Confirmed material issues are corrected and logged when appropriate.

This log records substantive editorial work, not cosmetic changes such as spacing, color or typography adjustments.

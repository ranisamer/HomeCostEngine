About

Useful numbers, clear assumptions.

HomeCostEngine is being built as an independent planning resource for U.S. homeowners.

Our approach

We prefer interactive tools that expose their assumptions. When a calculator cannot know something—such as your exact local labor rate or hidden site condition—we say so rather than presenting false precision.

What comes next

The roadmap includes more calculators, state and city cost guides, quote comparison tools and optional connections to local professionals. Monetized placements should be clearly disclosed and kept separate from estimator logic.

About Us

Helping homeowners plan projects with clearer numbers.

HomeCostEngine is an independent home-improvement planning resource for U.S. homeowners. We build free calculators and practical guides that help turn rough project ideas into more structured budgets, quantities and contractor questions.

Last reviewed September 21, 2026

Our mission

Home improvement planning often starts with a simple question—“How much will this cost?”—but the answer depends on much more than one national average. Project size, material choice, site access, labor conditions, regional pricing, hidden repairs and local requirements can all change the final number.

Our goal is to make those variables easier to understand before a homeowner requests bids or commits to a project. We focus on useful planning ranges, transparent assumptions and calculators that show how changing an input changes the estimate.

What you can use HomeCostEngine for

HomeCostEngine includes planning tools and educational resources for projects such as roofing, HVAC replacement, concrete, painting, flooring, remodeling and general home-maintenance budgeting.

Use the site to estimate quantities, test cost scenarios, compare contractor quotes and understand which parts of a project should be confirmed locally. Start with our home project calculators, browse the cost guides, or explore the planning library.

How our estimates work

We prefer visible assumptions over a single unexplained price. Depending on the tool, an estimate may use measurements, material selections, project complexity, market adjustments, waste allowances or other user-selected inputs.

When a browser-based calculator cannot know something—such as concealed damage, exact local labor pricing, permit requirements or site-specific structural conditions—we identify that limitation instead of presenting false precision.

Read our Cost Methodology for more detail on how planning ranges, regional adjustments and project assumptions are handled.

Our editorial approach

Every guide should answer a specific homeowner question and help the reader take a practical next step. We aim to explain calculations, distinguish general guidance from project-specific advice and link to stronger primary or authoritative sources when they materially support a technical point.

We review and improve content as calculators change, better sources become available or a page needs more context. Our Editorial Policy explains our approach to sourcing, updates, corrections and usefulness.

Advertising and commercial relationships

HomeCostEngine may use advertising, sponsorships, affiliate links or lead-generation partnerships to support the site. Commercial relationships should remain separate from calculator logic and should not change the mathematical assumptions used to generate a planning estimate. Relevant commercial relationships should be disclosed where applicable.

What HomeCostEngine is not

HomeCostEngine does not inspect properties and does not provide contractor bids, engineering services, code approval, legal advice or professional trade services. Our tools are intended for early-stage planning and education.

Final project scope and pricing should be verified with qualified local professionals who can inspect the property, confirm measurements, review applicable codes and permits, and price the actual work.

Corrections, feedback and partnerships

If you notice a calculation issue, outdated information, broken link or unclear assumption, we want to know. We also welcome relevant partnership and media inquiries.

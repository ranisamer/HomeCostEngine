What Is a Roofing Square?

Roofers often talk in squares instead of square feet. One roofing square is simply 100 square feet of roof surface.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

If a roof has 2,000 square feet of actual surface area, it contains 20 roofing squares before waste. Divide roof area by 100 to convert square feet to squares.

Why contractors use roofing squares

Squares provide a convenient unit for materials and labor. Shingles, underlayment and installation quantities are often discussed relative to the number of roofing squares.

Add waste after calculating the base squares

Hips, valleys, ridges and cuts create waste. Use the Roofing Square Calculator to apply a waste percentage and estimate bundles.

How many bundles are in a square?

Many shingle products use approximately three bundles per square, but packaging varies. Always check the exact product specification before purchasing.

Roof footprint is not always roof surface area

Slope increases surface area. If you are starting with horizontal footprint dimensions, use the Roof Pitch Calculator to understand the pitch multiplier, then build your budget with the Roof Replacement Cost Calculator.

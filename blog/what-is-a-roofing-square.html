What Is a Roofing Square?

Roofers often talk in squares instead of square feet. One roofing square is simply 100 square feet of roof surface.

Updated September 21, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

A roofing square is a quantity unit equal to 100 square feet of roof surface. Roofers and suppliers use squares because they make material and labor quantities easier to discuss than very large square-foot numbers.

Conversion: roofing squares = roof surface area ÷ 100.

Worked example

A roof with 2,240 square feet of measured surface area contains 22.4 roofing squares before any waste allowance.

ItemCalculationResult
Measured roof area—2,240 sq ft
Base squares2,240 ÷ 10022.4 squares
With 10% planning allowance22.4 × 1.1024.64 squares

Roofing square is not the same as house square footage

A 2,000-square-foot home does not automatically have a 20-square roof. Story count, footprint, pitch, overhangs, attached garages and roof geometry all change the roof surface area.

How pitch affects surface area

A steeper roof has more surface area than its horizontal footprint. If you only know the building footprint, use the Roof Pitch Calculator or a measured roof report before converting to roofing squares.

Why waste is separate from base squares

Waste can come from valleys, hips, ridges, starter courses, cut pieces and complex layouts. Keep the measured quantity separate from the allowance so you can see exactly how much extra material was added.

Bundles per square are product-specific

Many asphalt shingle products are packaged so a certain number of bundles cover approximately one square, but the exact bundle count and stated coverage should come from the manufacturer. Do not use a universal bundle assumption when ordering a specific product.

Use the roofing square calculator

The roofing squares and shingle bundle calculator converts measured roof area into squares, adds a selected waste allowance and lets you enter the actual bundles-per-square value from the product packaging. It also includes quick-reference results for common roof sizes from 1,000 to 5,000 sq ft.

What to ask before ordering

  • Is the roof area based on measured surface area or house footprint?
  • What waste allowance is being used, and why?
  • How many bundles per square does this exact product require?
  • Are starter, ridge and hip materials ordered separately?
  • Are underlayment, flashing and ventilation quantities included?

FAQ

How many square feet are in one roofing square?

100 square feet of roof surface.

Is 30 squares a 3,000-square-foot house?

Not necessarily. Thirty roofing squares means 3,000 square feet of roof surface, not living area.

Should I buy material based only on squares?

No. Use the product coverage instructions and account for accessories, starter, ridge/hip materials, underlayment and project-specific waste.

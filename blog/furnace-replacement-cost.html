Furnace Replacement Cost: What to Budget in 2026

Understand furnace replacement cost in 2026, including equipment size, efficiency, fuel, venting, electrical work, ductwork and installation scope.

Updated September 21, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

A furnace replacement quote should be read as a system proposal, not a price for one metal cabinet. Capacity, fuel type, venting, return and supply airflow, electrical work, controls, permits and startup can all change the installed scope.

Do not automatically replace like-for-like capacity

The old furnace size may reflect an old rule of thumb, previous insulation levels or equipment that was oversized from the beginning. Ask how the replacement capacity was selected and whether the contractor considered the home's current heating load.

Fuel and venting can change the project dramatically

Scope areaWhat to verify
FuelGas, oil or electric equipment and any supply changes
VentingExisting chimney, new venting or condensing-system exhaust
CondensateDrain route, pump or neutralization where applicable
Combustion airHow required air and safe operation are addressed
ElectricalBreaker, disconnect, wiring and control power

Efficiency upgrades can create installation work

Higher-efficiency equipment can require different venting, drainage or controls. Compare the complete installed package rather than assuming the efficiency rating is the only difference between two furnaces.

Airflow problems do not disappear with a new furnace

If the home has noisy returns, weak rooms or uneven temperatures, ask whether the duct system and filter arrangement are part of the evaluation. A new blower connected to a restrictive system can still deliver poor comfort.

What a useful proposal should name

  • Exact furnace model and capacity
  • Efficiency rating
  • Thermostat and controls
  • Filter cabinet or media
  • Supply/return transitions
  • Venting and condensate work
  • Electrical work
  • Permit, disposal and startup
  • Manufacturer and workmanship warranties

When to compare a heat pump or dual-fuel option

If the home already needs major replacement work, it can be useful to ask for an alternative heat-pump or dual-fuel proposal where those systems are practical. Compare electrical capacity, climate performance, backup heat and duct requirements—not just equipment price.

Model the broader budget in the HVAC Replacement Cost Calculator and use Heat Pump Installation Cost Factors if you are considering a system change.

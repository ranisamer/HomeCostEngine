Roof Replacement Checklist for Homeowners

Use the same checklist with every contractor so you can compare proposals on equal terms.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

A roof replacement starts with planning. Written scope and clear assumptions reduce the chance that one bid looks cheaper simply because it leaves out work.

Establish your planning range

Estimate roof area and use the Roof Replacement Cost Calculator before requesting bids.

Request itemized scope

Ask for tear-off, disposal, underlayment, flashing, drip edge, ventilation, roofing material, fasteners, cleanup and permits to be identified.

Clarify decking repairs

Ask how damaged decking will be charged and whether the bid includes an allowance.

Confirm product and warranty details

Get exact manufacturer and product line names. Separate material warranty from contractor workmanship warranty.

Discuss jobsite logistics

Confirm delivery, dumpster placement, landscaping protection, access, weather delays and cleanup.

Compare the same scope

Use the roofing quote comparison guide before selecting a bid.

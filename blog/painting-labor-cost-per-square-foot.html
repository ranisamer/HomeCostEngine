Painting Labor Cost per Square Foot in 2026

Interior painting labor is commonly quoted by project, hour, wall area or floor area—so the measurement basis must be clear before comparing prices.

Published September 15, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

A broad U.S. planning range for interior painting labor is approximately $1–$3 per square foot of paintable surface. Quotes expressed by floor area can look higher, often around $3–$7 per square foot, because wall and ceiling surfaces are not the same as the home’s floor footprint.

Confirm which square footage the painter uses

Paintable wall area is calculated from wall length and height, less large openings. Floor area is simply the room footprint. A $2-per-square-foot quote based on walls is not directly comparable with a $4-per-square-foot quote based on floor area.

Quick labor examples

If a room has 500 square feet of paintable wall surface, a rough $1–$3 labor allowance produces $500–$1,500 before ceilings, trim or extensive repairs. For a 2,000-square-foot home quoted by floor area, a broad $3–$7 method suggests $6,000–$14,000 for labor and possibly materials, depending on what the contractor includes.

Preparation can dominate the labor budget

Moving and covering furniture, protecting floors, filling holes, repairing drywall, sanding, caulking, stain blocking and priming all add time. Ask the painter to separate routine preparation from significant repair work.

Coats and color changes matter

A similar-color refresh may need less work than covering a dark or saturated color. New drywall, stains and uneven surfaces may require primer. The quote should state the assumed number of primer and finish coats.

Ceilings, trim and doors need separate measurements

Baseboards, crown molding, windows, doors, cabinets and detailed trim are slower than open wall areas. Confirm whether each surface is included and which finish will be applied.

Occupied homes and difficult access add time

High ceilings, stairwells, limited parking, daily cleanup, furniture movement and room-by-room scheduling can increase labor. An empty home with clear access is normally faster to complete.

How to compare painting quotes

  • Use the same rooms and surfaces for every quote.
  • State wall, ceiling, trim and door scope separately.
  • Specify preparation and repair assumptions.
  • Record primer and finish-coat counts.
  • Confirm whether paint, supplies, cleanup and disposal are included.

Frequently asked questions

How much is painting labor per square foot?

A broad planning range is about $1–$3 per square foot of paintable surface. Quotes based on floor area use a different measurement and can appear higher.

Is paint included in labor cost per square foot?

Sometimes, but not always. Ask whether the quote includes paint, primer, supplies, preparation, cleanup and taxes.

Why do two painters quote different square footage?

One may measure wall surface while another uses floor area as a pricing shortcut. Ask both contractors to state the measurement basis and included surfaces.

Roof Flashing Cost in 2026: Per Linear Foot, Chimney, Vent, Valley and Skylight

Flashing is not one product with one price. Edge metal may be measured in linear feet, while chimney, skylight, valley and vent details are better treated as separate assemblies.

Updated September 24, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial • U.S. homeowner planning guide

Quick answer: current 2026 HomeGuide data places many minor roof flashing repairs around $200–$500, with larger repairs or complete flashing replacement around $500–$1,500+. Location matters: a vent boot, valley, chimney and skylight are different jobs. See HomeGuide's current flashing cost guide.

Build a line-item estimate: use the Roof Flashing Cost Calculator to enter linear flashing plus separate chimney, vent and skylight allowances. This is more useful than forcing every detail into one average cost per foot.

Why “roof flashing cost per linear foot” can be misleading

Some components really can be priced by length. Drip edge and certain continuous metal details often use linear feet. But chimney flashing may involve base flashing, step flashing, counterflashing, masonry cuts and sealant. A skylight may use a manufacturer-specific kit. A vent boot is usually priced per penetration. Converting all of these to one linear-foot figure hides the actual scope.

NRCA defines flashing as part of the roof system used at joints and valleys to prevent water seepage. Its consumer information is a useful reminder that flashing is a core roof-system component, not decorative trim.

Chimney flashing: why one number is not enough

Ask whether the proposal includes step flashing along the sides, apron flashing at the downslope face and counterflashing integrated with masonry where applicable. A contractor quoting “chimney flashing” may mean a localized sealant repair; another may mean removal and rebuilding of the full assembly. Those are not comparable scopes.

Vent boots and pipe penetrations

HomeGuide's broader 2026 roof-repair data lists vent replacement in the low hundreds of dollars in many cases, but a vent flashing repair on a steep or difficult roof may cost more. Use a per-penetration allowance in the calculator, then ask whether surrounding shingles, underlayment and decking repair are included.

If multiple penetrations are involved, the total can become meaningful even though each individual boot is small.

Valley flashing

Valleys carry concentrated water flow and may use different systems depending on the roof covering and design. A quote should identify whether the valley is being repaired, fully replaced or converted to a different system. It should also clarify underlayment and leak-barrier work below the visible metal or shingles.

Skylight flashing

Skylight cost can be confusing because “flashing” may refer to a kit, adjacent roofing, curb work or full skylight replacement. Keep the flashing line separate from the skylight unit unless the contractor clearly bundles them.

Flashing repair vs flashing replacement

A small reseal is not the same as replacing corroded or poorly integrated metal. HomeGuide's 2026 article distinguishes minor repairs from major replacement, which is exactly how a homeowner should read bids. If one contractor recommends sealant and another recommends reconstruction, ask each to document the observed condition and expected life of the repair.

Do not use caulk as a substitute for understanding the detail

Sealants can be part of roof details, but a recurring leak at a wall, chimney or penetration may indicate a flashing integration problem rather than “not enough caulk.” The correct repair depends on the assembly. Avoid DIY decisions based only on where an interior stain appears because water can travel before becoming visible indoors.

Example line-item flashing budget

Suppose a reroof includes 100 linear feet of continuous metal at $3/ft, one chimney at a $750 allowance, two vent boots at $225 each and no skylights. The flashing calculator produces a $1,500 planning allowance. That result is only as good as the entered quantities and unit rates—but it is far easier to audit than a single “miscellaneous flashing: $1,500” line.

How flashing connects to underlayment and decking

Flashing is installed as part of a water-shedding system, not as an isolated metal patch. Use the Roof Underlayment Cost Calculator to keep the underlying water-management layer visible, and use the Roof Decking Replacement Cost Calculator if deterioration is found around a penetration.

Roofing quote checklist: flashing

  • Which existing flashing is reused and which is replaced.
  • Locations: valleys, sidewalls, headwalls, chimneys, skylights, pipes and edge metal.
  • Material type and finish where relevant.
  • Whether counterflashing or masonry work is included.
  • Whether skylight manufacturer kits are new or reused.
  • Whether surrounding shingles and underlayment are included.
  • How concealed deck damage around penetrations will be handled.
  • Whether sealant-only repairs are temporary or part of a full detail.

Frequently asked questions

How much does roof flashing cost per linear foot?

There is no universal flashing rate because many details are priced per assembly rather than per foot. Continuous edge or wall metal may use a linear-foot rate, while chimneys, vents and skylights are often priced individually.

How much does chimney flashing replacement cost?

Current consumer guides show a wide range because a small repair and full chimney flashing reconstruction are different jobs. Ask exactly which components and masonry work are included.

Is roof flashing replaced during a new roof?

Some flashing may be replaced and some may be reused depending on condition, system requirements and contractor scope. The proposal should identify the approach by location.

Can flashing leaks be fixed with sealant?

Sometimes sealant is part of a valid repair, but recurring leakage can indicate a failed flashing assembly. The cause should be identified before relying on a surface seal.

Should vent boots and skylight flashing be separate line items?

Separating them often improves quote comparison because each penetration can have different materials and labor.

Planning note: National ranges are useful for screening a budget, not for approving work. Replace assumptions with measured quantities, product specifications, local requirements and itemized contractor pricing.

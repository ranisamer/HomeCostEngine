How Much Concrete for a 12×12 Slab?

A 12×12 slab covers 144 square feet, so even a small thickness change affects total volume.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

At 4 inches thick, a 12×12 slab requires about 1.78 theoretical cubic yards. With a 10% waste allowance, the planning amount is roughly 1.96 cubic yards.

Measure inside the forms

Use the actual finished dimensions rather than relying on a rough project name such as “12×12.”

Choose thickness for the project

Patio, shed, equipment and vehicle slabs can have different design requirements. Verify the design before ordering concrete.

Add waste deliberately

Waste helps cover normal variation such as uneven subgrade and small placement losses. It should not replace accurate measurement.

Run several scenarios

Change thickness and waste percentage in the Concrete Calculator before asking suppliers for delivery quotes.

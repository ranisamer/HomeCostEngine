How Many Shingles Do I Need?

Shingle quantity starts with actual roof surface area, then converts to roofing squares and bundles.

Updated September 21, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

Shingle quantity should begin with measured roof surface area, not the home's living area. Convert the roof surface to roofing squares, add a justified allowance for cuts and layout, then use the exact manufacturer's package coverage.

Step 1: calculate roofing squares

One roofing square equals 100 square feet of roof surface. A measured 2,100 sq ft roof equals 21.0 base roofing squares.

Step 2: add a planning allowance

Base roof areaWasteAdjusted squares
2,100 sq ft5%22.05
2,100 sq ft10%23.10
2,100 sq ft15%24.15

The correct allowance depends on geometry, valleys, hips, dormers, starter layout and product instructions. Do not use a fixed percentage automatically.

Step 3: use exact package coverage

Some asphalt shingles are packaged so three bundles cover approximately one roofing square, but this is not universal. Heavier or specialty products can use different package counts. Always verify the manufacturer's current coverage information.

Worked bundle example

If a verified product uses 3 bundles per square and your adjusted quantity is 23.10 squares, the planning quantity is 69.3 bundles before rounding to whole packages. Final ordering should also account for supplier packaging and return policies.

Pitch can change the measured surface area

If you are starting from a horizontal footprint, slope increases surface area. Use the Roof Pitch Calculator before converting to roofing squares.

Do not mix field shingles with accessories

  • Starter shingles or starter strip
  • Ridge and hip cap
  • Underlayment
  • Ice/water protection where required
  • Flashing
  • Drip edge
  • Fasteners
  • Ventilation components

Use the roofing square calculator

The Roofing Square Calculator separates base squares, waste-adjusted squares and bundles so each assumption is visible.

Ordering checklist

  1. Verify measured roof surface area.
  2. Confirm roof geometry and pitch.
  3. Choose a justified allowance.
  4. Confirm the exact product and package coverage.
  5. Order accessories separately.
  6. Review supplier return and restocking policies.

FAQ

How many bundles are in one roofing square?

It depends on the product. Three bundles is common for many products, but use the exact manufacturer's coverage information.

Can I use my home's square footage?

No. Living area and roof surface area are different measurements.

Should I buy exactly the calculator result?

Use it as a planning quantity, then verify package coverage, accessories and job-specific waste with the installer or supplier.

Planning limit: This guide supports material planning only. Verify roof measurements, system details and product coverage before ordering.

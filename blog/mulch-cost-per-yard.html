Mulch Cost Per Yard: Delivery, Depth and Bed Size

Plan mulch cost per cubic yard using landscape area, depth, bulk material price, delivery and installation assumptions.

Updated September 21, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

Quick answer: a mulch price “per yard” usually refers to one cubic yard of bulk material. Your delivered project cost can also include delivery minimums, distance, placement, bed preparation and the quantity needed at your chosen depth.

Start with volume, not price

Measure the actual landscape bed and choose the additional depth you need. Cubic yards = area in square feet × depth in inches ÷ 324.

500 sq ft bedApprox. mulch volume
2 in deep3.09 yd³
3 in deep4.63 yd³
4 in deep6.17 yd³

Bulk price and delivered price are different numbers

A supplier may quote the material by cubic yard and add delivery based on distance, minimum load or truck type. Ask for the delivered total for your actual quantity rather than comparing yard prices alone.

Material type changes both price and appearance

Shredded hardwood, bark, dyed wood, pine products and specialty mulches can be sold at different prices and may age differently. Compare product description, particle size and color—not just the generic word “mulch.”

Existing mulch can reduce the order

If a bed already has material in place, measure the remaining layer before ordering a full new depth. Adding three more inches to a bed that already has several inches can create an unnecessarily thick layer.

Installation can cost more than the material

Bed edging, weed removal, landscape fabric removal, hauling bags through limited access and hand-spreading all add labor. If a contractor quotes installed mulch, ask whether the price includes bed preparation and cleanup.

Bagged vs bulk

Bagged mulch can be convenient for small quantities or difficult-access areas. Bulk delivery is usually easier for larger beds. Compare the labeled bag volume with the cubic-yard requirement rather than comparing one bag price with one yard price directly.

Use the Mulch Calculator to test depth and quantity, then read How Much Mulch Do I Need? before ordering.

Best supplier question: “What is the delivered total for my quantity, including minimums and fees?”

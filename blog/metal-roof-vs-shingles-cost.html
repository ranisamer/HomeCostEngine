Metal Roof vs Shingles Cost: What Changes the Price?

A metal roof and a shingle roof are different systems, so comparing only the headline material price misses much of the project.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

The better comparison is a complete installed scope: exact product, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, tear-off, labor and warranty.

Material systems differ

Metal panels, trim and fasteners are not equivalent to shingles, starter, ridge materials and accessories. Ask for exact product names in both proposals.

Installation labor differs

Panel type, penetrations, valleys, roof geometry and edge details can change installation time and skill requirements.

Tear-off must be matched

Make sure both quotes make the same assumption about existing roofing removal, deck condition and disposal.

Think beyond upfront price

Expected ownership period, maintenance, appearance, climate and insurance considerations may matter alongside initial cost.

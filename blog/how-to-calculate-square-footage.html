How to Calculate Square Footage

Square footage is one of the most useful starting numbers in home improvement. Once the area is correct, many material calculations become much easier.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

For a rectangular area, multiply length by width. A room that measures 20 feet by 15 feet has 300 square feet of floor area.

Rectangle and square formulas

For rectangles, use length × width. For a square, multiply one side by itself. Use the Square Footage Calculator if you want the result converted automatically to square yards and acres.

How to calculate a circular area

Measure the diameter, divide it by two to get the radius, then use π × radius². Circular patios, fire-pit areas and landscape beds are common reasons to use this formula.

What about irregular rooms?

Break the floor or yard into simple shapes. Calculate each rectangle, square or circle separately, then add the areas. This makes your measurement easier to verify.

Why square footage matters

Flooring, paint, mulch, roofing and many other materials begin with area. After measuring, use the Flooring Cost Calculator or Mulch Calculator for the next step.

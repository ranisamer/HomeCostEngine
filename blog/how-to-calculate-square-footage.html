How to Calculate Square Footage

Square footage is one of the most useful starting numbers in home improvement. Once the area is correct, many material calculations become much easier.

Updated September 21, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

Square footage is simply area measured in square feet. The most reliable approach is to break a space into simple shapes, calculate each area separately and then add the pieces together.

Rectangle and square

Formula: length × width.

Example: a 20 ft × 15 ft room = 300 sq ft.

Triangle

Formula: base × height ÷ 2.

Example: a 10 ft base × 8 ft height = 40 sq ft.

Circle

Formula: π × radius².

Example: a circular patio with a 6 ft radius has about 113.1 sq ft of area.

L-shaped or irregular rooms

Divide the room into rectangles, calculate each one, then add the totals. This is usually more accurate and easier to audit than estimating one rough overall dimension.

Worked L-shaped example

SectionDimensionsArea
Main rectangle14 × 12 ft168 sq ft
Extension6 × 4 ft24 sq ft
Total—192 sq ft

Gross area versus net material area

Some projects use gross area, while others subtract openings. Flooring usually starts with floor area. Wall paint starts with wall area and may subtract doors/windows depending on the estimating method. Roofing uses actual roof surface rather than house floor area.

Add waste only after measuring accurately

Flooring, tile and some other materials may need an allowance for cuts, pattern matching and damaged pieces. Keep measured area separate from the allowance so the estimate is transparent.

Common measurement mistakes

  • Mixing inches and feet without converting
  • Using outside building dimensions for an interior finish area
  • Forgetting alcoves, closets or extensions
  • Subtracting openings twice
  • Using house square footage as roof square footage
  • Adding waste before confirming the true measured area

Use the square-footage calculator

Use the Square Footage Calculator for rectangles and combined spaces, then move to a material-specific calculator for flooring, paint, roofing or landscaping.

FAQ

How many square feet is a 12 × 12 room?

144 square feet of floor area.

Do I include closets?

Include any area receiving the material or finish you are estimating.

Is wall square footage the same as floor square footage?

No. Wall area depends on wall lengths and height, while floor area depends on plan dimensions.

Planning limit: Field conditions and project-specific measurement conventions can vary. Measure the area that actually receives the material.

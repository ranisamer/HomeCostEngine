Concrete Cost Per Yard: What the Quote Really Includes

The supplier's price per cubic yard is only one part of a concrete project budget.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

Concrete pricing varies by local market and mix specification. Instead of relying on a static national number, get a current supplier quote and use that real price in your calculation.

Base ready-mix price

Mix strength, additives, fibers and other specifications can change the per-yard material price.

Short-load and minimum charges

Small deliveries can carry fixed charges because the truck and batch plant still incur setup and delivery costs.

Delivery and access

Distance, waiting time and inability to discharge near the forms can add costs such as pumping, buggies or extra labor.

Installed price is broader

Excavation, base preparation, reinforcement, forms, placement, finishing, joints and curing are separate from the ready-mix material price.

How Much Does It Cost to Paint a Room?

Build a room-painting budget from wall area, paint quantity, coats, prep, trim and labor instead of relying on one national average.

Updated September 8, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

The cost to paint a room depends on the size of the walls, number of coats, surface condition, paint choice and whether the project includes ceilings, doors, trim or repairs. A transparent estimate starts by measuring what will actually be painted.

Calculate wall area first

Use the Paint Calculator to enter room length, width, wall height, openings and coats. This gives you a material starting point rather than a guess based only on floor square footage.

Choose the number of coats

A maintenance repaint in a similar color may have different coverage needs than a dramatic color change or new drywall. Follow product instructions and realistic surface conditions.

Add preparation

Nail holes, cracks, peeling areas, stains, damaged corners and failed caulk can increase labor. Ask whether prep is included or priced separately.

Decide which surfaces are in scope

Walls, ceiling, baseboards, crown, doors, closets and window trim are separate surfaces. A room quote can look inexpensive simply because some of those items are excluded.

Price your actual paint

Enter the gallon price for the product you plan to use. For quantity planning, read How Much Paint Do I Need for a Room?

Compare contractor quotes consistently

If hiring a painter, compare prep, coats, paint brand and line, protection, cleanup and included surfaces. See Interior Painting Cost Per Square Foot for a broader framework.

Planning note: HomeCostEngine estimates are for budgeting. Verify final measurements, product requirements, permits, code requirements and contractor scope locally before purchasing or hiring.

Frequently asked questions

What information do I need to estimate painting cost?

Room dimensions, wall height, openings, coats, paint price, surface condition and the list of surfaces being painted.

Why do two painters quote the same room differently?

Preparation, product, number of coats, trim, protection, repairs and cleanup may differ between scopes.

Can I estimate paint material separately from labor?

Yes. Calculate gallons and paint cost first, then evaluate labor and prep as separate parts of the project.

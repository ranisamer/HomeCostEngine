How Much Concrete for a 20×20 Slab?

A 20×20 slab covers 400 square feet, so measurement and thickness errors can become expensive quickly.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

At 4 inches thick, a 20×20 slab has a theoretical volume of about 4.94 cubic yards. Adding 10% waste gives a planning quantity of about 5.43 cubic yards.

Larger pours need better logistics

At this volume, truck access, crew size, placement sequence and finishing time become important planning issues.

Confirm slab design first

Thickness should reflect intended loads, soil, base preparation, reinforcement and local practice.

Ask about delivery charges

Supplier distance, minimums, waiting time and discharge access can change the delivered cost beyond the headline price per yard.

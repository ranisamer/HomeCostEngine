Commercial HVAC Replacement Cost Estimator Guide

Commercial HVAC cost is usually planned by system capacity and scope, not building square footage alone.

Published September 15, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

A broad 2026 planning allowance for many light-commercial replacements is about $2,000–$5,000 per ton installed. A cross-building industry survey reported an average near $2,873 per ton, but project-specific access, controls and code work can move the result substantially.

Start with capacity and system type

List the tonnage of every unit and identify whether the project uses packaged rooftop units, split systems, heat pumps, variable refrigerant flow or central equipment. A simple five-ton rooftop replacement is a different scope from replacing several zones in an occupied building.

Quick per-ton planning method

Multiply total system tonnage by an early allowance of about $2,000–$5,000 per ton. For example, a 10-ton project produces a preliminary $20,000–$50,000 range. Treat that as a screening estimate only; it does not replace a site survey, load calculation or written contractor scope.

Equipment is only one part of the budget

A complete quote may include removal, disposal, curb adapters, economizers, duct transitions, electrical work, disconnects, thermostats or building controls, refrigerant piping, startup and testing. Compare these items separately.

Roof and crane access can change the bid

Rooftop work may require a crane, street or parking closures, lift plans, roof protection and coordination with property management. Restricted work hours or an occupied business can add labor and scheduling cost.

Efficiency and refrigerant requirements matter

New equipment may require changes to electrical service, controls, ventilation or refrigerant components. Confirm the applicable federal, state and local efficiency, refrigerant and mechanical-code requirements with the project engineer and contractor.

Budget for business interruption

Restaurants, medical spaces, data rooms and customer-facing locations may need temporary cooling or phased replacement. Include rental equipment, after-hours work and lost-operating-time risk in the project plan.

Commercial quote comparison checklist

  • Exact manufacturer, model, capacity and efficiency
  • Quantity of units and served zones
  • Crane, rigging, curb and roof work
  • Electrical, controls and duct transitions
  • Permits, inspections, testing and balancing
  • Warranty, commissioning and maintenance requirements

Sources and methodology

  • Principia Consulting — average commercial HVAC system cost
  • U.S. EPA — refrigerant transition and management
Planning note: These are preliminary U.S. budgeting ranges, not a contractor quote. Local labor, access, code, permit, equipment and finish requirements can materially change the final price. Compare written local quotes using the same scope.

Frequently asked questions

How much does commercial HVAC replacement cost per ton?

A broad early planning range is approximately $2,000–$5,000 per ton installed, but system type, access and project scope can move the final quote outside that range.

Does the per-ton estimate include a crane?

Not necessarily. Crane, rigging, permits, street closure and rooftop coordination should be confirmed as separate scope items.

Can building square footage determine commercial HVAC size?

Square footage can support a rough screening estimate, but proper capacity requires a professional load calculation using occupancy, envelope, ventilation, equipment and local climate.

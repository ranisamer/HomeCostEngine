Cost to Replace Ductwork in a 2,000 Sq. Ft. House

A 2,000-square-foot home may need roughly 160–240 linear feet of ductwork, but layout and access matter more than floor area alone.

Published September 15, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

For early planning, many whole-home duct replacement projects fall around $2,800–$10,000, while unusually difficult or extensive work can exceed that range. The useful estimate starts with duct length and installation conditions—not only the home’s square footage.

Quick planning range for a 2,000 sq. ft. home

A practical starting assumption is approximately 160–240 linear feet of supply and return ductwork. At a broad installed allowance of about $12–$40 per linear foot, the preliminary total is roughly $1,900–$9,600 before unusual repairs or system changes. Many complete projects cluster around $2,800–$10,000.

Estimate by linear feet, not floor area alone

Two homes with the same floor area can have different duct lengths. A compact single-story plan may use shorter runs than a multi-level home with several branches, returns and difficult chases. Ask the contractor to state estimated linear feet and the number of supply and return points.

Material changes the installed price

Flexible duct can reduce installation time where permitted and appropriate. Sheet metal and rigid fiberglass systems may require more fabrication and labor. Insulation level, sealing method, hangers, boots, registers and transitions must be included in a fair comparison.

Access is often the biggest labor variable

An open basement or accessible attic is usually simpler than a cramped crawlspace or concealed route behind finished ceilings. Drywall opening and repair, asbestos investigation, mold remediation and damaged framing should appear as separate scope items.

Repair, seal or replace?

Minor disconnected sections or accessible air leaks may be repairable. Widespread deterioration, crushed flexible runs, poor sizing or contamination can justify broader replacement. Ask for diagnostic evidence rather than assuming every old duct must be removed.

Do not combine equipment and ductwork into one line

If the project also includes a furnace, heat pump or AC replacement, request separate equipment, ductwork, electrical, controls and permit lines. Use the HVAC Replacement Cost Calculator to plan the equipment side, then compare the combined total.

Questions to ask in each quote

  • How many linear feet and air outlets are included?
  • Which duct material and insulation rating are specified?
  • Are removal, disposal, sealing and balancing included?
  • Will walls or ceilings be opened and repaired?
  • Are permits, testing and commissioning included?

Frequently asked questions

How much ductwork does a 2,000 sq. ft. house need?

A rough starting assumption is 160–240 linear feet, but the number of floors, returns, branches and equipment location can change the actual length.

Can I replace ductwork without replacing the HVAC system?

Yes, when the existing equipment is suitable and the new ducts are designed to match its required airflow and capacity.

Does duct replacement include drywall repair?

Not automatically. Ask whether opening and restoring walls or ceilings is included or handled by another contractor.

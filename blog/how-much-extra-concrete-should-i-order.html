How Much Extra Concrete Should I Order?

Ordering exactly the theoretical volume leaves no room for ordinary field variation.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

The geometric calculation is the starting point. Real forms and subgrade are not perfectly uniform, and supplier order increments can change the practical quantity.

Where extra volume goes

Low spots, uneven excavation, bowed forms and small placement losses can increase actual concrete consumption.

Accurate measurement comes first

Waste should cover normal variation, not compensate for uncertain dimensions.

Supplier increments matter

The amount you can order may be rounded differently from the exact calculated cubic yards.

Compare waste scenarios

The Concrete Calculator lets you test 5%, 10% and 15% waste allowances before confirming the quantity.

Verify important pours

For large or time-sensitive pours, confirm the final order with the contractor or ready-mix supplier.

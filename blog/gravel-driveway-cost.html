Gravel Driveway Cost per Square Foot: 2026 Planning Guide

Build a realistic gravel driveway budget by separating stone, delivery, excavation, grading, drainage and compaction instead of relying on material price alone.

Updated September 17, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

A gravel driveway budget is driven by more than a price per ton. Driveway dimensions, required depth, stone type, existing base, grading, drainage, delivery and compaction all influence the project.

Calculate material quantity first

Measure the driveway area and use the Gravel Driveway Cost Calculator to estimate cubic yards, approximate tons, material, delivery and site-preparation cost for the selected depth.

Depth changes the order quickly

Increasing depth increases volume directly. A driveway receiving a new surface layer is a different project from building a new driveway base from native soil.

Material type affects weight and purpose

Crushed stone, limestone, pea gravel and other aggregates can have different densities and performance characteristics. Confirm the supplier's material recommendation and tons-per-yard conversion.

Delivery can be a separate cost

Supplier minimums, delivery distance and truck access can change the delivered price. Ask whether quoted material pricing includes delivery, fuel charges or minimum-load fees.

Site prep can exceed material cost

Excavation, geotextile, grading, drainage, base construction and compaction may be required before the visible gravel layer is placed. A material-only estimate should not be treated as an installed driveway price.

How much does a 100-foot gravel driveway cost?

A 100-foot driveway that is 12 feet wide covers 1,200 square feet. At a 4-inch compacted depth, the theoretical quantity is about 14.8 cubic yards before an order allowance. Multiply the confirmed tons or yards by local material and delivery prices, then add excavation, grading, base, drainage and compaction. Divide the completed planning total by 1,200 square feet to compare it with installed quotes on the same basis.

Using #57 limestone in the gravel calculator

Select limestone or use the supplier's density for #57 stone when available. Density varies with quarry source, moisture and gradation, so confirm the supplier's tons-per-cubic-yard factor before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

Is gravel driveway cost just price per ton?

No. Quantity, delivery, excavation, grading, drainage, base work and compaction can all be important parts of the project.

Why does gravel depth matter so much?

Volume increases directly with depth, so a thicker layer requires more material over the same area.

Should I use tons or cubic yards?

Project dimensions produce volume, while suppliers may sell by weight. Use both and confirm the supplier's conversion for the actual material.

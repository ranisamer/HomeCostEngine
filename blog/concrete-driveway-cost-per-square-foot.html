Concrete Driveway Cost Per Square Foot: 2026 Planning Guide

Estimate concrete driveway cost per square foot by understanding thickness, base preparation, reinforcement, finish, access and ready-mix delivery.

Published September 11, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

Concrete driveway cost per square foot combines much more than wet concrete. Excavation, grading, base material, forms, reinforcement, thickness, finish, joints, access and disposal can all affect the installed total. A useful budget separates the concrete quantity from the labor and site work.

Measure the driveway accurately

Measure the full length and width, then divide irregular areas into simple rectangles. Include aprons, turnarounds and widened parking sections. Enter the measurements and planned thickness in the Concrete Calculator to estimate cubic yards rather than relying only on a square-foot price.

Thickness changes material quantity

A small increase in slab thickness adds concrete across the entire driveway. Vehicle weight, soil, climate, drainage and local requirements can influence the design. Review the factors in the concrete driveway thickness guide, then confirm the final section with a qualified local contractor.

Base preparation may drive the budget

Concrete performs best when the support below it is suitable for the site. Removing an old driveway, excavating weak soil, importing aggregate, grading and compacting the base can add substantial work. A low bid may assume minimal preparation, so ask each contractor to describe the base depth and compaction process.

Reinforcement, joints and finishing

Rebar or welded wire, control joints, isolation joints and thickened edges may be specified for the project. Finish options can range from a standard broom texture to color, exposed aggregate or decorative borders. Confirm whether sealing and a return visit for saw cutting are included.

Delivery and placement conditions

Ready-mix suppliers may charge for short loads, waiting time, special mixes or difficult scheduling. If the truck cannot reach the forms, wheelbarrow labor or a concrete pump may be needed. Use the Cubic Yard Calculator to double-check the volume before discussing delivery.

Plan a sensible order allowance

Forms and subgrade are not perfectly uniform, and some concrete remains in the chute or equipment. Ordering the theoretical volume exactly can create a costly shortage during the pour. Read how much extra concrete to order, then confirm supplier increments and the contractor’s recommendation.

Compare ready-mix with bagged concrete

Bagged material may work for a very small repair, but a driveway usually requires continuous placement and consistent mixing. The ready-mix versus bags comparison explains the labor, access and delivery trade-offs.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in concrete driveway cost per square foot?

It may include concrete and labor, but excavation, base work, reinforcement, demolition, permits and sealing can be separate.

Does a thicker driveway cost more?

Yes. Greater thickness increases concrete volume and may also change excavation, forms and reinforcement.

Should I choose the lowest square-foot quote?

Only after confirming that thickness, base, reinforcement, finish, joints, disposal and warranty are equivalent.

How to Choose a Roofing Contractor Without Comparing Price Alone

The lowest roofing bid is not automatically the best value. Contractor qualification and written scope matter before price.

Updated September 9, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

A roof is a system, and the installation quality matters as much as the product label. Prequalify contractors before you compare final totals.

Verify the business and coverage

Ask for business information, licensing where required, liability coverage and workers’ compensation information that applies through the project period.

Request a detailed written proposal

The proposal should identify roofing material, tear-off, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, cleanup, start and completion expectations, payment terms and warranty.

Check references and recent work

Ask for recent projects similar to yours and speak with customers about communication, cleanup, change orders and warranty follow-up.

Understand supervision and quality control

Ask who will oversee the crew, who your day-to-day contact is and how installation issues are documented.

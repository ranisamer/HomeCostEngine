Concrete Patio Thickness: What Should You Consider?

There is no single patio thickness that fits every property and every load.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

The slab design should reflect intended use and the conditions beneath the concrete. A thicker slab cannot automatically compensate for poor base preparation or drainage.

Start with intended use

Pedestrian furniture loads are different from hot tubs, heavy outdoor kitchens or equipment.

Base and soil matter

Excavation, drainage, compaction and granular base influence how the slab is supported.

Climate and local practice matter

Freeze-thaw exposure, expansive soils and local requirements may change the recommended assembly.

Calculate volume after design

Once thickness is selected appropriately, use the Concrete Calculator to convert patio length, width and thickness into cubic yards.

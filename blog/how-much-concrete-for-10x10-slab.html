How Much Concrete for a 10×10 Slab?

A 10×10 slab covers 100 square feet; thickness determines the volume.

Updated September 7, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

At 4 inches thick, a 10×10 slab has a theoretical volume of about 1.23 cubic yards. With a 10% waste allowance, the planning quantity is about 1.36 cubic yards.

Thickness is the key variable

The same 100-square-foot slab uses more concrete as thickness increases. Choose thickness based on use, soil, base, reinforcement and local requirements rather than quantity alone.

Use actual form dimensions

Enter length, width and thickness in the Concrete Calculator. Small differences in forms or subgrade can affect the final order.

Compare ready-mix and bags

For a pour this size, compare supplier delivery fees and short-load charges against the labor of mixing many bags.

Verify before ordering

Supplier increments and site conditions can change the practical quantity. Confirm the final order with the supplier or contractor.

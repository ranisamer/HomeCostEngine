## Requested download: immediate, transactional

Subject: Your free Home Project Cost Planning Guide

Your guide is ready. Download the PDF and start with the scope worksheet. Write down the work you want, the quantities you know and the items that still need inspection. [Download the free guide](https://homecostengine.com/assets/guides/home-project-cost-planning-guide.pdf).

Keep the guide beside your quotes. It is a planning resource, not a contractor proposal or engineering review. This delivery is implemented in /api/ebook; do not configure a duplicate automatic welcome email.

## Learning email: two days after enrollment

Subject: Before comparing prices, match the scope

Two proposals can cover different work even when they name the same project. Check removal, preparation, specified material, permits and restoration. Mark each exclusion instead of assuming it is included.

[Compare contractor quotes](https://homecostengine.com/calculators/contractor-quote-comparison?utm_source=learning-email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=planning-course&utm_content=scope). Bring one real proposal, then add the next when it arrives.

## Learning email: four days after enrollment

Subject: Separate known work from your contingency

A known excluded repair belongs in the project subtotal. A contingency is a reserve for uncertainty, not a substitute for pricing work you already know is required. Record what is known, what is an allowance and what still needs inspection.

[Update your project contingency](https://homecostengine.com/calculators/project-contingency?utm_source=learning-email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=planning-course&utm_content=contingency). Use your own budget and chosen reserve. No percentage fits every property.

## Learning email: seven days after enrollment

Subject: One useful check before choosing a roof proposal

For a roofing project, confirm whether the measured area is roof surface or house floor area. Keep material, tear-off, flashing and repair assumptions beside the number. If roofing is not your current project, choose the relevant tool from the calculator library instead.

[Review your roof planning scenario](https://homecostengine.com/calculators/roof-replacement?utm_source=learning-email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=planning-course&utm_content=review) or [choose another project tool](https://homecostengine.com/calculators/).

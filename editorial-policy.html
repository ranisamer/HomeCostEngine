Editorial Policy

How HomeCostEngine writes, reviews, updates and corrects home improvement cost content.

Reviewed September 14, 2026 • HomeCostEngine Editorial

Our purpose

We publish practical planning tools that help U.S. homeowners understand project quantities, cost drivers and questions to ask local professionals. Content is educational and should never be interpreted as a substitute for an inspection, engineered design, code review or contractor proposal.

Content standards

Every substantive guide should answer a defined planning question, explain important assumptions, identify major exclusions and connect to a relevant calculator where available. Cost examples should use transparent arithmetic and be labeled as illustrative. We avoid invented customer stories, fabricated contractor quotes and unsupported guarantees.

Review and updates

A visible review date indicates when content was last checked. Reviews consider calculation logic, scope completeness, broken links, clarity, safety and whether price language is appropriately qualified. Pages may be updated when materials, standards, reader feedback or site tools change.

Safety and professional boundaries

Home projects can involve electricity, gas, heights, structural systems, permits and hazardous materials. Articles should not encourage unqualified work or bypass local requirements. When a task has meaningful safety or code implications, readers are directed to qualified local professionals and authorities.

AI-assisted production

AI and automation may assist drafting, formatting and illustrative image creation. Published material must still meet the same accuracy and transparency expectations. Generated images are illustrative, not records of actual projects or evidence of pricing.

Corrections

Readers can report an issue through our contact page. Helpful reports include the page URL, disputed statement, calculator inputs and a supporting source. Confirmed material errors are corrected and review dates may be updated.

Advertising independence

Advertising may support access to free tools. Advertisers do not set calculator formulas or editorial conclusions. Any future sponsored or affiliate material should be clearly disclosed on the relevant page.
